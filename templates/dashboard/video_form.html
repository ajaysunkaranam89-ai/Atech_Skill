About video protection

YouTube unlisted links are not access control — anyone who obtains the link can watch the video. Use them for convenience only.

Uploaded MP4s are stored in private storage and streamed only after the server checks that the viewer is enrolled (or is an administrator).

For high-value paid content in production, use a protected video service with signed, expiring URLs and DRM (e.g. Bunny Stream, Mux, Vimeo OTT, Cloudflare Stream, VdoCipher) via the External HTTPS URL option.
